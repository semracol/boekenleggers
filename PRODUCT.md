# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers, book-club members, students, library enthusiasts, and people looking for a small, personal gift. The current site explicitly targets shoppers in Belgium and the Netherlands; the customer profile is based on existing project copy and has not been independently validated.

## Product Purpose

Present illustrated bookmarks as small gifts and useful reading companions. Success on this page is helping visitors browse the designs, understand the prices and delivery terms, and add products to the demo cart.

## Positioning

Four illustrated bookmark designs are presented as cheerful, giftable keepsakes for readers.

## Operating Context

The site is a static GitHub Pages storefront. Visitors can browse products, add and adjust items in a browser-persisted cart, view delivery thresholds, read FAQs and legal information, and contact the shop through WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site currently shows four bookmark designs at €4.50 each.
- Shipping is presented as €2.50 per order for fewer than five bookmarks and free from five items.
- A five-bookmark gift box is shown at €22.50; its demo action adds five copies of the first design.
- The listed delivery area is Belgium and the Netherlands.
- Cart and cookie preferences are stored locally in the browser.
- Checkout is a demo and does not take payment or submit orders.
- Business and legal details remain placeholders in the legal content and must be completed and reviewed before real sales.

## Brand Commitments

The existing shop name is Boekenleggers. The supplied redesign reference is an interactive hanging-badge rail; adapt its flip-through interaction to the real bookmark designs rather than importing its fictional team members, external portraits, React, Tailwind, or dependencies.

- The existing HTML, JavaScript, README, and update notes describe the current product list, prices, delivery terms, demo cart, privacy behavior, and legal placeholders.

## Product Principles

- Keep product designs and their real imagery central.
- Make prices and shipping conditions easy to find.
- Keep demo cart and checkout behavior honest.
- Do not fabricate reviews, company details, or fulfillment capabilities.
